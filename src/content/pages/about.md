I founded Optimus Codeworks to help small businesses get custom software that fits how they work. I have experience building inventory managment systems, with a focus on custom designs to incorperate seamlessly with current workflows.

My approach is simple: understand the problem first, build only what's needed, and leave clients with software they can rely on and understand. I'd rather show than tell, so most projects on this site include demonstrations of previous projects for you to browse.
